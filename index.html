FAQ

The important details, without the fine-print maze.

Need a custom contract, bulk delivery or deeper Estonia-specific enrichment? Talk to us directly.

What does PropData Estonia provide?

A normalized API over official-source Estonian address and cadastral records, including property identity, available parcel geometry, buildings, explicit relationships, match status and provenance.

Why are prices shown in EUR and USD?

Estonia self-serve plans are billed in EUR. USD values are approximate reference conversions for U.S. buyers; the actual converted amount can vary with exchange rates and card-network fees.

Do you infer missing official property facts?

No. Source facts are fail-closed: if a field is unavailable or unverified, it remains null or explicitly unavailable rather than being manufactured.

Can I use Estonia alongside other PropData countries?

Yes. Estonia is available as a dedicated commercial surface while remaining part of the broader PropData international property graph. Global and custom contracts are available for multi-country workflows.
